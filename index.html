About Me

I’m a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Bulacan State University. My studies sit between two areas I enjoy: building software and understanding the networks it runs on.

On the software side I work with programming, system design, and database management. On the networking side I’m learning core concepts and how to troubleshoot them. I like turning ideas into working interfaces, and I’m keen to explore network setups and virtualized server environments as I learn.
